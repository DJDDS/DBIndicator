Regime & market filter

Two ways to adapt the screener to current conditions instead of using one fixed rule all the time - both are additive, nothing here changes what "Aligned" itself counts.

Used to classify each stock as Trending/Ranging/Transitional (shown in the Regime column).
In a Ranging regime, Relative Volume must clear threshold × this multiplier instead of the plain threshold - stricter confirmation for choppier conditions.
When on, a row whose direction disagrees with NIFTY 50's own current direction loses its "Confirmed" status (and Positional Qualified / High Conviction) - counter-trend trades have historically had a lower win rate. Off by default.
When on, a row whose direction disagrees with its own recent Chaikin Money Flow reading (the small ▲/▼ badge next to Volume - is recent volume actually buying or selling pressure, not just magnitude) loses its "Confirmed" status. Off by default.
When on, a row whose direction disagrees with its own most recent candlestick pattern (the small candle badge next to Signal - Engulfing/Hammer-family/Morning-Evening Star, a read of price SHAPE rather than a smoothed derivative like RSI/MACD/EMA-BB) loses its "Confirmed" status. Off by default.
When on, a row whose direction disagrees with its own NSE sectoral index's current direction (e.g. a Bullish HDFCBANK row while NIFTY BANK itself reads Bearish) loses its "Confirmed" status. A symbol without a known sector mapping is never blocked by this. Off by default.
When on, a row whose direction is decisively against the watchlist's own current advance/decline split (below the threshold % to the right) loses its "Confirmed" status. This reads breadth from your WATCHLIST, not the full NSE market - Kite has no cheap full-market breadth feed. Off by default.
Minimum % of the watchlist's resolved rows that must share a row's own direction for breadth to "agree" with it. Only changes anything if Require breadth agreement (above) is on.

Risk management

This app never touches your funds, positions, or orders - Account capital is a number YOU tell it, used purely to turn the ATR stop above into a suggested share quantity (fixed-fractional position sizing: risk exactly this % of capital if the stop is hit). Set it to your real trading capital for the Qty suggestion on the Dashboard to mean anything. The daily/position limits below feed an informational banner on the Dashboard, computed from trades YOU logged in the Signal Journal today - nothing here blocks you from logging another trade past a limit, it just tells you plainly when you have.

Fixed-fractional sizing: 1-2% is the typical starting point for F&O, tighter than cash equities.
Commonly ~3x your per-trade risk. The Dashboard banner turns red once today's logged trades exceed this.
A cap on simultaneously-open journal trades ("portfolio heat") - too many at once defeats per-trade sizing.

Risk layer (ATR stop/target)

A suggested stop-loss and target shown next to each row's Close price, sized to that stock's own recent volatility (ATR) instead of a flat percentage. Display only — this app never places an order.

Lookback (in bars) for the Average True Range calculation.
Suggested stop = entry −/+ this × ATR (against direction).
Suggested target = entry +/− this × ATR (with direction). Default 1.5/3.0 = 1:2 risk-reward.
