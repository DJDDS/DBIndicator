Signal rule

Out of RSI, MACD, Chaikin Money Flow and Relative Volume - how many must agree. Default is 4-of-4: fewer, higher-conviction candidates. Note the watchlist scans DAILY candles now; the intraday panel on the dashboard is separate and always 15-min + 4-hour.
Multiple of the 20-bar average volume needed to count as a match.

Regime & market filter

Two ways to adapt the screener to current conditions instead of using one fixed rule all the time - both are additive, nothing here changes what "Aligned" itself counts.

Used to classify each stock as Trending/Ranging/Transitional (shown in the Regime column).
In a Ranging regime, Relative Volume must clear threshold × this multiplier instead of the plain threshold - stricter confirmation for choppier conditions.
When on, a row whose direction disagrees with NIFTY 50's own current direction loses its "Confirmed" status (and Positional Qualified / High Conviction) - counter-trend trades have historically had a lower win rate. Off by default.
When on, a row whose direction disagrees with its own most recent candlestick pattern (the small candle badge next to Signal - Engulfing/Hammer-family/Morning-Evening Star, a read of price SHAPE rather than a smoothed derivative like RSI/MACD) loses its "Confirmed" status. Off by default.
When on, a row whose direction disagrees with its own NSE sectoral index's current direction (e.g. a Bullish HDFCBANK row while NIFTY BANK itself reads Bearish) loses its "Confirmed" status. A symbol without a known sector mapping is never blocked by this. Off by default.
When on, a row whose direction is decisively against the watchlist's own current advance/decline split (below the threshold % to the right) loses its "Confirmed" status. This reads breadth from your WATCHLIST, not the full NSE market - Kite has no cheap full-market breadth feed. Off by default.
When on, a row whose MACD histogram is shrinking against its own direction (momentum fading rather than accelerating - not just which side of zero it's on, which macd_line/signal_line already covers) loses its "Confirmed" status. Off by default.
Minimum % of the watchlist's resolved rows that must share a row's own direction for breadth to "agree" with it. Only changes anything if Require breadth agreement (above) is on.

Risk management

This app never touches your funds, positions, or orders - Account capital is a number YOU tell it, used purely to turn the ATR stop above into a suggested share quantity (fixed-fractional position sizing: risk exactly this % of capital if the stop is hit). Set it to your real trading capital for the Qty suggestion on the Dashboard to mean anything. The daily/position limits below feed an informational banner on the Dashboard, computed from trades YOU logged in the Signal Journal today - nothing here blocks you from logging another trade past a limit, it just tells you plainly when you have.

Fixed-fractional sizing: 1-2% is the typical starting point for F&O, tighter than cash equities.
Commonly ~3x your per-trade risk. The Dashboard banner turns red once today's logged trades exceed this.
A cap on simultaneously-open journal trades ("portfolio heat") - too many at once defeats per-trade sizing.

Risk layer (ATR stop/target)

A suggested stop-loss and target shown next to each row's Close price, sized to that stock's own recent volatility (ATR) instead of a flat percentage. Display only — this app never places an order.

Lookback (in bars) for the Average True Range calculation.
Suggested stop = entry −/+ this × ATR (against direction).
Suggested target = entry +/− this × ATR (with direction). Default 1.5/3.0 = 1:2 risk-reward.

Anticipatory signals (catch the move before it happens)

RSI/MACD/EMA-BB/CMF are all confirmatory - they tell you a move is already under way. These four are the app's ANTICIPATORY reads instead, aimed at BTST/swing and catching a big move early: two are pure display badges (no direction of their own, so they can't gate anything), two have real gates like every other "Require ... agreement" checkbox above.

How many recent bars to rank current Bollinger Band width against. Display only (the "Coiling" badge) - a coiled stock can break either direction, so this never gates Confirmed.
Current BB width must rank at or below this percentile of the lookback window to count as "coiling" (default 20 = tightest 20% of recent readings).
A bar's own true range must be at least this many times its own ATR to count as a range-expansion "big candle".
Shared by the big-candle read and the standalone BTST strong-close read below: close must land in the top/bottom (100 − this)% of the bar's own range to count as extreme. Default 80 = top/bottom 20%.
When on, a row whose most recent qualifying big candle (within the last 15 bars) disagrees with this row's own direction loses its "Confirmed" status. Off by default.
When on, a row whose own close did NOT land in the extreme top/bottom of its own range, in its own direction, loses its "Confirmed" status - a BTST "closed with conviction" gate. Off by default.
Minimum NSE delivery % (most recently published session, NOT live - see status below) to count as "agreeing". Absolute floor, not stock-relative - a known simplification.
When on, a row whose delivery % reading is below the threshold above loses its "Confirmed" status. A symbol with no delivery data available is never blocked by this. Off by default.
How far past its own VWAP price may already be, in its own direction, before the entry counts as EXTENDED (chasing rather than catching early). Default 2.0.
When on, a row that's already extended past the threshold to the left loses its "Confirmed" status - filters out late/chasing entries specifically. Off by default.
A stock whose ATR is below this % of its own price isn't moving enough to plausibly deliver a big move. Expressed as a % so one threshold works across cheap and expensive stocks. Default 0.5%.
When on, a row below the ATR floor to the left loses its "Confirmed" status - screens out genuinely dead names regardless of how many parameters agree. Off by default.
