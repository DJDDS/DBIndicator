OI shows today's Open Interest with its Structure badge underneath (Long/Short Buildup = fresh positions being added, Short Covering/Long Unwinding = positions being closed, based on today's price move vs today's OI move since session open) plus today's OI % change. OI Accel compares this scan's OI move to the previous scan's - Accelerating/Stable/Weakening/Transitional/Flat - with ⚠ unusual flagging a move much larger than that stock's own recent swings. When OI is building in a direction and accelerating, that row also gets a bold ▲ Break Up / ▼ Break Down badge - a stronger, more decisive read than either signal alone. Vol shows the latest candle's actual traded volume plus that as a multiple of its recent average, highlighted orange at 1.5×-3× and red above 3×. A ✓ next to a Bullish/Bearish Signal pill means it's "Confirmed" - volume is above 1.2× its own 20-bar average, and (on 15-min scans) the 4-hour trend agrees too - added to cut down on false signals from noisy 15-min candles.
