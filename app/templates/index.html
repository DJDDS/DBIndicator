This page updates itself live in the background every 20 seconds (no full reload, so your filters, sort order, and scroll position stay put). OI = today's Open Interest with Structure badge (buildup/unwind vs today's open) and % change. OI Accel compares OI's move in the latest 30 minutes to the 30 minutes before that (in percentage points) - Strong/Moderate acceleration means it's building faster than its own recent pace; a bold Break Up/Down badge means the OI Structure agrees with that acceleration too. See the OI Screener for the full breakdown (15/30/60-min windows, Day OI Change vs yesterday's close). Vol = latest candle volume as a multiple of its recent average (orange 1.5–3×, red above 3×). Aligned is out of 4 parameters - RSI, MACD, Chaikin Money Flow (directional volume) and Relative Volume (magnitude) - each counted equally. CMF replaced the old EMA9-vs-Bollinger-mid vote, which was a third transform of the same close series; the vote is now 2 price reads + 2 volume reads, which is genuinely more independent evidence. "Match required" in the Settings popover sets how many of the 4 must agree for a row to count as Bullish/Bearish. A ✓ = "Confirmed": Aligned meets your Match required setting and (15-min scans) the 4-hour trend agrees too - Telegram alerts only fire for confirmed signals. Regime is an ADX-based read of whether this stock is currently Trending or Ranging/choppy (number in parens is the raw ADX value) - in a Ranging regime the Relative Volume bar automatically gets stricter (multiplier set on the Settings page) since breakouts there are more prone to false starts. A ⚠ next to Signal means this row's direction disagrees with the Market banner above (NIFTY 50's own current direction) - turn on "Require index/market agreement" on the Settings page to have disagreeing rows lose their Confirmed status automatically. Score is a backtest-informed alternative to the plain Aligned count - each of the 4 parameters is weighted by its own recent historical win rate instead of counted equally; run "Auto-Weight Parameters" on the Backtest page to compute real weights from your watchlist (defaults to equal 25%-each weighting, same as Aligned/4, until you do). Want a bigger-picture read? Switch Timeframe (top-left of the filter bar) to 4hour, or use the Backtest page. The small SL / TGT line under Close is a suggested stop-loss/target sized to this stock's own recent volatility (ATR) rather than a flat percentage - display only, tune the ATR length and multipliers on the Settings page. A 📓 badge next to Signal shows the REALIZED win rate from your own Signal Journal for rows sharing this exact (direction, aligned) setup - only appears once at least 5 of your own resolved paper trades share it, so it's never a misleading number from 1-2 trades. VWAP and anchored VWAP moved off this table to the chart page - Ext below already carries the actionable version of that distance. Qty under Close is a fixed-fractional position-size suggestion (risk a set % of your Account capital, set on the Settings page, if the SL is hit) - and the toolbar's Risk pill tracks trades YOU logged in the Signal Journal today against your own daily-risk/position/ sector-concentration limits. This app places no real orders and has no visibility into your broker account - both are suggestions computed from numbers you configured, not live account data. The small ▲/▼ badge next to MACD shows whether the histogram itself is rising or falling vs. the previous bar - momentum accelerating or fading, genuinely different from macd_line vs. signal_line (which just says which side of zero it's on); turn on "Require MACD histogram momentum agreement" on the Settings page to have a fading-momentum row lose Confirmed status. Under Close, a 🎯 Coiling badge means this stock's Bollinger Band width is near a multi-week low relative to its own history (NR7 = today's range is the narrowest of the last 7 bars) - tight, ANTICIPATORY reads (unlike RSI/MACD/EMA-BB/CMF, which are all confirmatory) that have historically preceded bigger breakouts more often than an already-wide range. A 💥▲/💥▼ badge is a "big candle" - a bar whose own range was a real multiple of its ATR AND closed near its own high/low - with the level it set and whether price has since continued through it (✓); turn on "Require big-candle agreement" on the Settings page to gate on it. Close@N% shows where the close landed within today's own high-low range (100% = at the high) - the BTST "closed with conviction" read; turn on "Require strong-close agreement" to gate on it. Close@ and NR7 are measured on the daily bar, which is where they carry their intended BTST meaning - the watchlist is pinned to daily precisely so these never silently describe something else. Deliv N% is NSE's delivery percentage (real overnight conviction vs. intraday churn) as of its most recently PUBLISHED session - never a live number, and it may never appear at all if NSE is blocking this deployment's requests (see the Settings page for status). Ext N R is how far price already is past its own VWAP in ATR units, measured in this row's own direction - negative means still early / pulled back, and an ⚠ means the move is already extended beyond your threshold (you'd be chasing rather than catching it early). ATR N% is this stock's ATR as a percentage of its own price - a stock below your configured floor is too quiet right now to plausibly produce a big move no matter how many parameters agree. Both have opt-in gates on the Settings page. The ⚡ Best Entries card at the top ranks those same confirmed rows by how good the ENTRY looks (entry location, big candle, volatility, coiling, strong close, delivery) rather than by how strong the signal is - hover any row there for the full score breakdown. Signals are directional input, not investment advice.
