Shows only stocks currently in one of the Dashboard's Parameter Screener tiers (matching 2, 3, or 4 of RSI/MACD/ EMA9-BB/Relative Volume right now) - not the full watchlist. Day OI Chg % = today's latest OI vs yesterday's last-seen OI (its closing value). 15m/30m/60m Chg % are rolling-window OI moves ending now - 30m is the primary early-alert window, 60m the stronger confirmation window, 15m the fastest and noisiest. Acceleration = the latest 30-minute OI change minus the previous 30-minute OI change, in percentage points: above +2.00 is Strong, +0.5 to +1.00 is Moderate, -0.30 to +0.30 is Stable, below -0.30 is Weakening, below -2.00 is Possible exit/unwinding. A stock can show a big whole-day OI number while actually unwinding in the latest window, so for a fresh entry this recent-direction read matters more than the cumulative one alone. OI Structure compares today's price move against today's OI move (both since session open): both up is fresh buying (Long Buildup), both down is longs exiting (Long Unwinding), price down with OI up is fresh shorting (Short Buildup), price up with OI down is shorts covering (Short Covering). This page refreshes itself roughly every 20 seconds - no need to reload.
