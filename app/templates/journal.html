Forward-testing signal journal

Click "Log" next to any live signal on the Dashboard to record it as a paper trade here. Entry fills at the NEXT bar's open after the bar you logged from (never that bar's own close - no lookahead), and it resolves once enough new bars have closed at your horizon - the exact same convention the Backtest page uses, so a trade logged here is a genuine like-for-like comparison against what a backtest on the same symbol/timeframe/params would have predicted. Resolution runs automatically in the background scan loop while the market is open - just leave this tab closed and check back later.

This scoreboard reports REALIZED forward performance only - it isn't automatically compared against a backtest number. To see how it stacks up, run the same watchlist/timeframe/parameters through the Backtest page over a matching date range.

Like every other piece of scan state in this app, the journal lives in a local file on the server - there is no persistent storage volume attached. A trade still marked Open at the moment of a redeploy will be lost; already-Resolved trades are safe. Use "Export CSV" below before deploying if you have open trades you don't want to risk.
