What this does

F&O Early Movement Research is the primary live-parity tool. It replays the same staged idea as the dashboard: Energy Building (compression/BB coil), Ignition (fresh momentum + participation), then Best Entry (OI, relative strength, VWAP/anti-chase and context). It reports 1/2/3/5/10-bar outcomes net of costs and keeps the latest 30% of events as chronological holdout.

The older gate sweep and custom confluence replay remain below only as diagnostics for comparing legacy ideas. Threshold sensitivity changes one parameter at a time, so future improvements can be evidence-led without brute-force fitting thousands of combinations to the same history.

2 · Legacy gate audit

Runs a baseline with every gate off, then one pass per gate with only that gate on, and reports what each did to expectancy and profit factor — net of costs, on your own watchlist. Start here: it measures the filters you're actually running instead of assuming more filters are better.

Each gate is tested alone plus a few targeted OI combinations. A 30% chronological holdout is kept untouched and is the primary ranking column, so a gate that only looks good in-sample does not float to the top. Gates that cannot apply to your scope are skipped rather than reported as "no change".

3 · Does the BTST/STBT premise hold?

The overnight panel picks stocks that closed at the extreme of their range on a day that went their way, and holds them overnight. Nothing in this app could measure that trade until now — the normal research tools measure 1/2/3/5/10-bar moves and enter at the next executable bar, while an overnight idea must enter at the signal close to measure the gap itself.

This enters at the signal bar's own close and exits at the next open and the next close — the trade as you would actually take it. It applies only the two hard gates, not the soft checks, and automatically compares the original continuation direction with the exact opposite reversal direction. If the sign is wrong, the research says so instead of trying to rescue it with more gates.

Worth knowing what you might find: this setup buys the most extended point of the session, and short-horizon reversal is a well-documented effect — momentum tends to work over months, while over one to five days strong recent returns often give back. If the win rate lands meaningfully below 50%, the premise is inverted rather than merely weak, and no amount of tightening the other checks fixes it. A result near 50% after costs means there is no edge here either way.
