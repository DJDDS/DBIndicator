What this does

Replays the exact "High-Conviction Setups" rule from the dashboard - strict 3-of-3 confluence, OI structure agreeing with direction, an actively accelerating OI break signal, volume at 1.5× average or higher, price on the right side of VWAP, and the separate 4-hour trend agreeing - bar by bar over real historical price and Open Interest data from your Kite connection. It reports what would actually have happened: real win rate, average return, and worst drawdown per trade, at each horizon below.

Read before trusting the numbers: Kite's instrument list only contains the CURRENTLY ACTIVE futures contract, so historical Open Interest realistically only goes back 30-45 days regardless of how many days you ask for - this caps how many trades the backtest can find. A small trade count (single digits, or zero) is a real, honest result for a filter this narrow - it is not a bug, and it also means the win rate isn't statistically reliable yet. Past performance never guarantees future results, and this is not investment advice - use it to sanity-check the rule, not as a promise of returns.
