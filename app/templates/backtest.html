What this does

Replays whichever parameters you pick below - bar by bar over real historical price data from your Kite connection. An entry fires the moment at least your chosen "Required" count of them agree on the same direction at once. Deliberately does not factor in Open Interest or VWAP - this tests your own combination of indicator and volume conditions on its own, separate from the broader "High-Conviction" filter on the dashboard. Results are broken out for Bullish and Bearish setups separately, since a strategy's edge often differs by direction.

Read before trusting the numbers: picking fewer required out of more selected parameters fires often and can produce hundreds of trades even over a short window - a high trade count makes the win rate more statistically meaningful, but still isn't a guarantee of future performance. Requiring most or all of your selected parameters fires far less often, so don't be surprised by a small trade count there. This is not investment advice - use it to sanity-check the rule, not as a promise of returns.

Auto-Weight Parameters

Backtests each of the live screener's 4 parameters over your watchlist to measure which have actually been predictive lately - RSI Cross, MACD Cross, and EMA/BB Cross each solo (required=1), and Relative Volume as a confirmation LIFT (win rate of the 2-of-3 baseline's trades where volume was also hot at entry, vs where it wasn't) since volume has no direction of its own to test solo. Converts the result into weights for the dashboard's Score column - a backtest-informed alternative to the plain Aligned/4 equal-weight count. Takes a few minutes (4 full backtest passes over your watchlist). This measures one recent window on your specific watchlist, not a permanent verdict on an indicator - re-run periodically rather than treating a single result as final.

Pick exactly one. A NIFTY 50/SENSEX run measures how RSI/MACD/EMA-BB have predicted moves on the index itself - Relative Volume's weight will read low/neutral for these since indices report 0 volume on Kite.
