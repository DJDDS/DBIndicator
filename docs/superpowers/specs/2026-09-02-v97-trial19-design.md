# V9.7 Trial 19 Design

## Goal
Validate the nonlinear extreme total-FUTSTK-OI event on a third, older, untouched NSE history window without changing the V9.5.3 discovery or V9.6 Trial-17 definitions.

## Frozen hypothesis
- Trial 19 event: `total FUTSTK OI z >= 1.5`.
- Evidence window: 2018-09-01 through 2021-08-31.
- Primary horizon: next-session 1D horizon-scaled ATR movement.
- Secondary 2D cannot rescue 1D.
- Directionless, research/shadow only.

## Primary baseline and inference
- Baseline is eligible non-event F&O observations from the same trading date and the same DTE bucket (0-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21+).
- Event rows are never included in the baseline.
- Primary lift must be >=1.10 and the day-cluster bootstrap 95% lower bound must exceed 1.00.
- Binary `extreme_oi_event` coefficient is tested with two-way clustered standard errors by trading date and symbol; t-stat must be >=3.0.
- Minimum 250 events and 250 event days.
- Top-3-day-removed matched lift must remain >1.00.
- At least 3 of 4 chronological matched-lift blocks must be >1.00.
- Top-five symbols must not dominate; report concentration without post-hoc exclusion.

## Integrity
- Point-in-time historical F&O membership from actual FUTSTK presence.
- Official NSE historical cash OHLC.
- Share-equivalent OI normalization and actual DTE.
- MWPL/ban fail-closed; primary inference excludes ban/95%+ MWPL observations.
- No current-universe substitution.

## Promotion control
- Earnings +/-5 trading-session exclusion is required before Trial 18 can become eligible for preregistration.
- It cannot rewrite Trial 19.
- Trial 18 does not auto-run.

## Safety
- Prior finals remain unread/locked.
- `ACTIVE_PLAYBOOKS = ()` unchanged.
- No TRADE/WATCH activation.
