# V9.6 Trial 17 Design

## Objective
Independently validate the V9.5.3 exploratory finding that fresh total stock-futures OI expansion predicts larger next-session movement.

## Frozen hypothesis
- Trial: 17
- Event: total share-equivalent FUTSTK OI daily percentage-change z-score >= 1.5.
- Primary outcome: next-session movement in horizon-scaled ATR (1D).
- Secondary outcome: 2D; cannot rescue a failed 1D result.
- Directionless; no long/short call.
- Independent window: 2021-09-01 through 2023-09-01 inclusive.
- The V9.5/V9.4 discovery windows and every previously locked final remain unread and are not reused.

## Evidence gates
A PASS requires all of:
- >= 250 event rows and >= 100 distinct event trading days.
- 1D lift >= 1.10x.
- Day-cluster bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 1.00x.
- Cluster-robust t-stat on continuous total-OI z >= 3.0 after realized-volatility, ATR%, and DTE controls.
- Top-3-day-removed lift > 1.00x.
- At least 3 of 4 chronological blocks have lift > 1.00x.
- No missing historical membership or OI-normalization control.
- If efficacy passes but MWPL/ban coverage is missing, result is INCONCLUSIVE rather than PASS.

## Diagnostics only
Report, but do not tune on:
- 2D lift.
- DTE buckets.
- symbol concentration.
- abnormal unwind comparator.
- MWPL/ban populations when available.

## Production safety
- Research/shadow only.
- No production playbook activation.
- No Trial 18 direction research unless Trial 17 passes independent validation.
