## Dataset

- 210 pre-30-Sep NSE F&O underlyings
- 141 trading days: 2 Mar–25 Sep 2026
- 40 sessions calibration-only
- 101 sessions strictly out-of-sample
- Dhan 1-minute historical cash data
- NIFTY 50 plus 15 available DBIndicator sector-index series
- sector assignment in this 1-minute approximation is correlation-based, matching the auditor-style approximation rather than exact production symbol mapping
- no option bid/ask history is used, so no option-P&L claim is made

## Primary calibration result

The fixed production-like critical value |Z|=3.188815 is badly miscalibrated on the 1-minute approximation:

- trigger rate: **10.82%**
- events: **451,981**

Rolling empirical calibration using completed prior sessions only produced:

- mean q99 max-|Z|: **7.290**
- median q99 max-|Z|: **6.997**
- range: **6.543–9.349**
- empirical multiscale minute trigger rate: **1.34%**
- events: **62,660**

This independently confirms the auditor's main calibration concern. The auditor's retrospective 5.66 value was not hard-coded or reused.

### CUSUM ablation

Empirical + stock CUSUM:
- +15m: **-0.65 bps**, CI **[-1.33, -0.14]**

Empirical + stock + residual CUSUM:
- +15m: **-0.68 bps**, CI **[-1.36, -0.09]**

CUSUM reduces event count but does not create positive continuation in this OOS replay.

## Residualisation / horizon ablation

Every tested fixed horizon remains negative at +15m:

- 1m: -0.94 bps
- 2m: -0.89 bps
- 3m: -0.81 bps
- 5m: -0.82 bps
- 10m: -0.73 bps
- 15m: -0.70 bps

Five-minute variants:
- raw stock: **-0.66 bps**
- market residual: **-0.68 bps**
- market + sector residual: **-0.82 bps**

Therefore neither residualisation nor multiscale selection demonstrates a continuation edge on this 1-minute OOS sample.

## Economic hurdle

Empirical multiscale mean forward move after a 5/10/15-bps underlying hurdle:

- +15m after 5 bps: **-5.68 bps**
- +15m after 10 bps: **-10.68 bps**
- +15m after 15 bps: **-15.68 bps**

A positive mean sufficient to support option-buying friction is not demonstrated.

## Conditional model

Walk-forward L2 logistic continuation model, using causal detection-time features only:

- +5m AUC: **0.574**, Brier **0.231**
- +15m AUC: **0.516**, Brier **0.249**
- +30m AUC: **0.506**, Brier **0.256**

The +5m model contains weak ranking information, but even its top probability decile averaged only about **+0.33 bps**. At +15m and +30m the model is effectively near-random and not economically useful.

## Research conclusion

On 1-minute historical data, the V1.0/V1.1 price-only continuation architecture **does not demonstrate a tradeable continuation edge**. Empirical calibration fixes the over-firing problem, but it does not fix the outcome problem.

This result does **not** settle the exact production 5-second horizons (20–160 seconds), which remain untested historically. It does establish that:

1. the Normal/Bonferroni |Z|=3.188815 threshold should not be assumed calibrated;
2. simply raising the threshold is insufficient;
3. CUSUM is not enough to manufacture continuation;
4. residualisation and multiscale horizon selection do not earn their place on the 1-minute OOS evidence;
5. no production formula change should be made from this result alone.

## Next research gate

The next valid test is the exact §14 5-second/tick-level replay with contemporaneous option bid/ask. Until that exists, V1.0 should remain a research/paper-trade system rather than being promoted on the basis of statistical significance alone.
